TASTE OF HOME

African Immigrants’ proximity to African food stores in Massachusetts

My final project visualizes my study area, Boston, Massachusetts. I am interested in visualizing access to African food stores within Massachusetts. The map is a digital visualization to show store distribution within the Boston area.
