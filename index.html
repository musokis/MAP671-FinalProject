TASTE OF HOME

African Immigrants’ proximity to African food stores in Massachusetts

Project Overview

My final project visualizes my study area, Boston, Massachusetts. I am interested in visualizing access to African food stores within Massachusetts. The map is a digital visualization showing store distribution within the Boston area.The goal is to understand how these stores are frequented, as a way to maintain ties to their homelands.

Final Map

Map Details

The map was created with QGIS 4.2.2. For the final map, I used the EPSG: 26986 "NAD83 / Massachusetts Mainland" projection that the state of Massachusetts uses. Data was obtained from Google maps. https://www.google.com/maps The scale for the man map is 1:187343 and the inset map is 1:1498745. To get more details on how the map was created, click here to go to GitHub project repository. To view the map in a higher quality file, click here to go to higher resolution image of map.

Limitations

This map is only the begining of a hopefully more complex project on food and culture across the US. In the future, I would like to make this map an interactive map especially since some of the stores become more visible the more one zooms into the Boston metropolitan area. I also think it would be a good visual story if I am able to get population data differentiated by country of emigration. It would be great to visualize the patrons of each of these stores, their distribution within the state, in addition to fieldwork on how they maintain ties to their home countries.
