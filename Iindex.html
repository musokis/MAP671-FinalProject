TASTE OF HOME

African Immigrants’ proximity to African food stores in Massachusetts

My final project visualizes my study area, Boston, MA. I am interested in visualizing access to African food stores within Massachusetts. As such, the map in this project combines digital map visualization to show African immigrant population and their proximity to African food stores in the state.
